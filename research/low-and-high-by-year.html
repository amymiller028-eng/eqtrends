EQ & Hard Times

We’ve looked at EQ scores across periods that coincide with big events, from the 2008 recession to elections to COVID. Travel through time and click any star to see what we found.

What it means

Hard times show up in EQ scores, but not always the way you’d expect. Averages rarely move much from one year to the next. The bigger signals are at the edges. In 2008, fewer people showed high EQ skills. In 2016 and at the start of the COVID lockdowns, more people scored low, and in 2020 that eased again within a few months. EQ skills are learned, and they’re shaped by the people and circumstances around us.
